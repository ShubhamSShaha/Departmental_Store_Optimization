**Key findings**
- **Strongest association:** fromage blanc → honey (lift 5.2): customers buying one are 5x more likely than average to buy the other
- **Highest-volume pairing:** herb & pepper → paneer (1.6% of all baskets, lift 3.3), the most actionable rule for co-placement
- **Meal-kit pattern:** pasta pairs with escalope (lift 4.7) and shrimp (lift 4.5); whole wheat pasta with olive oil (lift 4.1)
- **5 customer segments** from 200 customers. The two that matter most:
  - *High income, high spend* (19.5% of customers; avg income $86.5K, spending score 82): priority for loyalty and premium offers
  - *High income, low spend* (16%; avg income $89.4K, spending score 16): the biggest upsell opportunity

**Recommendations**
- Place paneer near spices and sauces; bundle pasta with proteins as a "dinner kit" promotion
- Target the high-income, low-spend segment with personalized offers to shift their spending up
- Protect the high-income, high-spend segment with loyalty rewards rather than discounts
